# Code of Conduct

MacWarden is maintained by volunteers for people who run Lightning nodes. Everyone who takes part in the project, in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space, is expected to make that participation respectful and free of harassment, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and accept technical criticism constructively, with reasons and evidence.
- Respect people's time: search before asking, and keep reports focused and reproducible.
- Keep credentials and other people's private information out of every project space. Never post a real macaroon, `lndconnect://` URI, Nostr Wallet Connect string, TLS key or seed, your own or anyone else's.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal or discriminatory attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and deliberate disruption.
- Publishing others' private information, including credentials, node details or contact details, without their explicit permission.
- Encouraging or helping anyone to use a credential, node or wallet they are not authorised to use.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report a violation privately through the repository's private reporting form, <https://github.com/ZilverZtream/macwarden/security/advisories/new>, with "Code of conduct" in the title, or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile, [@ZilverZtream](https://github.com/ZilverZtream). Reports are kept confidential. Do not open a public issue for a conduct report.

Security vulnerabilities are reported the same private way; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and decides on a response proportionate to the behaviour: a private warning, removal of comments or contributions, a temporary ban from project spaces, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may face the same measures.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.
